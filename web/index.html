Curious little questions

Wait — is this safe? What does Pip actually see?

Pip does all his sniffing on your own computer. Your emails are never uploaded to us, and your gift-card codes are never stored anywhere — we only ever count them up. The scary part of the internet doesn't get to touch your money.

Do I have to connect my email?

Your choice. You can point Pip at a private export of your mail (fully offline), or give him a read-only peek — he can look, but never send, delete, or change a thing.

How much does it cost?

Finding your stash is free. If we ever charge, it's a tiny nibble only after Pip has actually dug up money for you. No found money, no fee. Fair's fair.

When can I actually use it?

Soon! Pip's still in training. Join the list and early diggers get first dibs the moment his claws are sharp enough.

Why a squirrel?

Because squirrels bury their treasure and forget exactly where. Sound like anyone you know? Pip's just better at remembering than the rest of us.
